Privacy Policy for Viewpoint

Last updated: September 2026

Viewpoint ("we", "the app") is a social discussion and debate app where people post topics, share opinions, and vote. This page explains what information we collect, why, and how it's handled.

Information we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive and store:

  • Your name (as provided by Google), used as your display name on posts
  • Your profile picture URL, used as your avatar
  • Your email address, used to identify your account

As you use Viewpoint, we also store the content you create: topics, opinions, replies, votes, and reports you submit. This content is tied to your account so it can be displayed, edited, or removed by you later.

We also store your theme preference (light/dark mode) locally in your browser. This is not sent to our servers.

How we use your information

We use this information only to operate the app: displaying your posts and profile to other users, sending you notifications (such as replies or mentions), enforcing one-vote-per-person rules, and letting you edit or delete your own content.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising or share it with marketers.

Who can see your information

Your display name, avatar, and any topics/opinions/replies you post are visible to other users of the app, since Viewpoint is a public discussion platform. Your email address is not shown publicly.

Third-party services we use

  • Google Sign-In — used to authenticate your account. Google's own privacy policy governs how they handle your sign-in data: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Supabase — our backend database and authentication provider, which stores your account and content data securely. Supabase's privacy policy: supabase.com/privacy
  • GitHub Pages — hosts the Viewpoint website itself as static files.

Data retention and deletion

Your content and account data are retained for as long as your account exists. If you'd like your account and associated data deleted, contact us at the email below and we'll remove it.

Children's privacy

Viewpoint is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
